# Deep Learning Book (MIT Press) 📖

**Authors:** Ian Goodfellow, Yoshua Bengio, Aaron Courville  
**Official Website:** [https://www.deeplearningbook.org/](https://www.deeplearningbook.org/)

The **Deep Learning textbook** is widely regarded as the "Bible of Deep Learning". It provides a comprehensive mathematical and conceptual foundation for machine learning and deep artificial neural networks.

---

## 🗺️ Table of Contents & Direct Online Reading Links

### Part I: Applied Math and Machine Learning Basics
* **Chapter 1:** [Introduction](https://www.deeplearningbook.org/contents/intro.html)
* **Chapter 2:** [Linear Algebra](https://www.deeplearningbook.org/contents/linear_algebra.html) — Scalars, vectors, matrices, tensors, matrix multiplication, eigendecomposition, SVD, Moore-Penrose pseudoinverse.
* **Chapter 3:** [Probability and Information Theory](https://www.deeplearningbook.org/contents/prob.html) — Random variables, probability distributions, marginal/conditional probability, Bayes' rule, entropy, KL divergence.
* **Chapter 4:** [Numerical Computation](https://www.deeplearningbook.org/contents/numerical.html) — Overflow/underflow, conditioning, gradient-based optimization, constrained optimization.
* **Chapter 5:** [Machine Learning Basics](https://www.deeplearningbook.org/contents/ml.html) — Learning algorithms, capacity, overfitting/underfitting, regularization, hyperparameters, maximum likelihood, supervised/unsupervised learning, stochastic gradient descent (SGD).

### Part II: Deep Networks: Modern Practices
* **Chapter 6:** [Deep Feedforward Networks](https://www.deeplearningbook.org/contents/mlp.html) — Multilayer Perceptrons (MLPs), gradient-based learning, hidden units, activation functions, backpropagation.
* **Chapter 7:** [Regularization for Deep Learning](https://www.deeplearningbook.org/contents/regularization.html) — Parameter norm penalties ($L^1$, $L^2$), dataset augmentation, noise robustness, early stopping, parameter sharing, dropout.
* **Chapter 8:** [Optimization for Training Deep Models](https://www.deeplearningbook.org/contents/optimization.html) — Empirical risk minimization, challenges in deep optimization (saddle points, vanishing/exploding gradients), algorithms with adaptive learning rates (RMSProp, Adam).
* **Chapter 9:** [Convolutional Networks (CNNs)](https://www.deeplearningbook.org/contents/convnets.html) — Convolution operation, pooling, variants of convolution, neuroscientific basis.
* **Chapter 10:** [Sequence Modeling: Recurrent and Recursive Nets (RNNs)](https://www.deeplearningbook.org/contents/rnn.html) — Unfolding computation graphs, recurrent neural networks, Bidirectional RNNs, Encoder-Decoder architectures, LSTM and GRU.
* **Chapter 11:** [Practical Methodology](https://www.deeplearningbook.org/contents/guidelines.html) — Performance metrics, default baseline models, determining whether to gather more data, tuning hyperparameters, debugging strategies.
* **Chapter 12:** [Applications](https://www.deeplearningbook.org/contents/applications.html) — Computer vision, speech recognition, natural language processing (NLP).

### Part III: Deep Learning Research
* **Chapter 13:** [Linear Factor Models](https://www.deeplearningbook.org/contents/linear_factors.html) — PCA, ICA, Slow Feature Analysis.
* **Chapter 14:** [Autoencoders](https://www.deeplearningbook.org/contents/autoencoders.html) — Undercomplete autoencoders, regularized autoencoders, denoising autoencoders, Contractive Autoencoders.
* **Chapter 15:** [Representation Learning](https://www.deeplearningbook.org/contents/representation.html) — Greedy layer-wise unsupervised pretraining, transfer learning, domain adaptation.
* **Chapter 16:** [Structured Probabilistic Models for Deep Learning](https://www.deeplearningbook.org/contents/structural_prob.html)
* **Chapter 17:** [Monte Carlo Methods](https://www.deeplearningbook.org/contents/monte_carlo.html) — Markov Chain Monte Carlo (MCMC), Gibbs sampling.
* **Chapter 18:** [Confronting the Partition Function](https://www.deeplearningbook.org/contents/partition.html)
* **Chapter 19:** [Approximate Inference](https://www.deeplearningbook.org/contents/inference.html) — Variational inference, Expectation Maximization (EM).
* **Chapter 20:** [Generative Models](https://www.deeplearningbook.org/contents/generative_models.html) — Variational Autoencoders (VAEs), Generative Adversarial Networks (GANs), Generative Moment Matching Networks.

---

## 🎯 Recommended Study Order
1. **Foundation:** Review Chapters 2, 3, 4, 5 thoroughly if you want to understand the mathematics behind neural networks.
2. **Core Practice:** Master Chapters 6, 7, 8, 9, 10 before diving into modern Transformer and LLM architectures.
3. **Generative Foundation:** Read Chapter 20 to understand how VAEs and GANs laid the foundation for modern Diffusion and autoregressive generative models.
